# POS & ERP Software for Business Operations

Bring Point of Sale, inventory, purchases, expenses and business reporting into one platform. Retail, wholesale and growing businesses can connect daily sales and stock records with the modules their teams need.

## Connect Sales, Inventory and Purchasing

Record counter sales and customer payments, review product quantities and manage supplier purchases. Business location settings and stock transfers help teams organize operations across branches.

Explore the [Point of Sale guide](/docs/sales-pos-guide), [inventory management guide](/docs/stock-management-guide) and [purchase management guide](/docs/purchases-guide).

## Extend Your Business Workflows

Accounting, CRM and Essentials modules support financial records, customer follow-ups and staff workflows. Other modules cover areas such as manufacturing, repair and online-store connections. Availability depends on your configuration and subscription.

Use the [module overview](/docs/modules-overview) to explore the available tools and the [reporting guide](/docs/reports-guide) to understand business reports.

## Get Started with the Right Setup

Review the [software documentation](/docs) for setup instructions and everyday workflows. [Contact the team](/c/contact-us) to discuss your business requirements and confirm the modules you need.
